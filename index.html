FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call or email us and we will respond.

What does EPCC mean?

Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Commissioning. We take responsibility for all four stages, so one team is accountable from design to handover.

Which sectors do you serve?

Oil & Gas, Power, Petro-Chemical, Cement, Automobile and Infrastructure.

Which licences and certificates do you hold?

PEC C1 contractor registration, OGRA registration, ISO 9001:2015 quality management, ISO 45001:2018 occupational health and safety, and SECP incorporation.

Do you offer plant maintenance and shutdown work?

Yes. We provide preventive and predictive maintenance, periodic shutdowns, annual turnarounds and unplanned repairs.

Do you manufacture equipment?

Our Lahore and Karachi workshops fabricate products including LPG bowsers, silos, conveyors, bucket elevators, process piping and penstocks.

Do you work outside Pakistan?

Our group has companies in Dubai and UAQ, UAE. Contact us with your project details and we will confirm what we can deliver.

How do I start a project?

Send us your scope, location and timeline by phone or email. We reply with next steps after reviewing your requirements.
